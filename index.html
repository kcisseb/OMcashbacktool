Assumptions
  1. Premium increases as a result of scheduled yearly cover increases are not considered.
  2. All premiums between cashback anniversaries have been paid and no premiums have been missed (due to a premium holiday or any other reason), waived (because the benefit, including premium protection, is in payment), reversed or refunded.
  3. The cashback feature takes on all the attributes of the product it's attached to, such as premium frequency, premium term and premium increases.
  4. No contract changes are made by you or us, as this may change your cashback.
Note: You may choose to add Cashback (at an extra cost) to any Old Mutual Protect product except Business Life Cover and Business Expenses Cover. Cashback is paid on each cashback anniversary if the contract and the product it is attached to are active on that date, and Cashback itself has not been removed from the contract before then. Each Cashback amount equals 15% of the total premiums received on the product since Cashback was added, or since the previous cashback anniversary. You have to remain with us and pay premiums for 5 years before you qualify for the benefit.
